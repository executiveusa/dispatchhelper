On Palm Beach island, parking can shut your job down.

Crew vehicles have to park on the private site. When the lot can't hold them, the Town allows street permits for at most three vehicles. Park outside that, and each violation is a strike.

Strike one

A crew truck on the street

Ticketed by Public Works or Police. The job now needs a construction management agreement with the Town.

Strike two

The sub who didn't get the memo

One late crew member is enough. Appeals must be filed in writing within seven days.

Strike three

Stop Work Order

The zoning director is required by code to issue it. Only the Town Council can restart the project.

With a shuttle

Zero crew cars on the street

Everyone parks at the staging lot and rides in. The Town's own management agreements name shuttles as the fix.

Source: Town of Palm Beach, construction parking and the 3-strike rule

Downtown West Palm Beach, too. For major projects, the City's conditions of approval can include off-site parking and shuttle operations, and it follows up with developers when subcontractors park in the neighborhoods.

Questions superintendents ask first.

Who handles rider IDs and the printed schedule?+
You do: your company issues any rider ID procedure and posts the schedule for your crew. We set the routes and times with you and adjust them together as the job changes.
How do you price it?+
Per service day, not per rider, based on the number of round trips. A fuel surcharge applies only when average fuel prices rise past set thresholds, and we document the prices whenever it does.
What if a vehicle breaks down?+
A replacement vehicle arrives within one hour of the report. If the day can't be completed, you get a daily credit or a make-up day on a date we agree.
Can the shuttle carry tools and materials?+
The shuttle is for people. Drivers don't load or unload packages or parcels for riders.
What happens when the job wraps early?+
Either side can end the agreement with 60 days' written notice. Any extra trips outside the regular route are booked separately.
Where do you operate?+
Palm Beach County, from our yard at 800 23rd Street in West Palm Beach.
