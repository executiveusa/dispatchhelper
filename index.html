Questions about the service

How is the service priced?+
Pricing is based on the route, service days, vehicle requirements and number of daily trips.
Do we need a staging lot before contacting you?+
No. If you already have one, include it in the request. If not, tell dispatch what you know about the site and crew.
Who sets the shuttle schedule?+
The schedule is built around the project's shift times and agreed with the site team.
Can the shuttle carry construction materials?+
The shuttle service is for passengers. Material or package handling is not part of the standard service.
What if the project schedule changes?+
Contact dispatch. Service days and trip times can be adjusted subject to the service agreement.
Where do you operate?+
Palm Beach County.
